# Top 10 Songs 2010


**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium


**Link:** [View Problem on Strata Scratch](https://platform.stratascratch.com/coding/9650-find-the-top-10-ranked-songs-in-2010)

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
Find the top 10 ranked songs in 2010. 
Output the rank, group name, and song name, but do not show the same song twice. 

Sort the result based on the rank in ascending order.

---

## 💻 SQL Solution

```sql

SELECT 
    year_rank,
    group_name,
    song_name
FROM(
SELECT DISTINCT
    DENSE_RANK() OVER(PARTITION BY year ORDER BY year_rank) as year_rank,
    group_name,
    song_name
from billboard_top_100_year_end
WHERE year = 2010
)t 
WHERE year_rank <= 10
