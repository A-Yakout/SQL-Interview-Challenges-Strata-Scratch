# Calculate Samantha's and Lisa's total sales revenue


**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy

**Topic:** SubQuery

**Link:** [View Problem on Strata Scratch](https://platform.stratascratch.com/coding/10127-calculate-samanthas-and-lisas-total-sales-revenue?code_type=1)

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
A sales operations team wants to check the combined revenue contribution of two top salespeople. 
Find the total sales revenue generated by Samantha and Lisa.

Output the total sales revenue as a single number.

---

## 💻 SQL Solution

```sql

SELECT
    SUM(sales_revenue) AS total_revenue
FROM (
SELECT 
    salesperson,
    sales_revenue,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER(ORDER BY sales_revenue DESC) rnk
FROM sales_performance
) t 
WHERE rnk <= 2
